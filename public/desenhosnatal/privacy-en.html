Privacy Policy - Christmas Coloring Book

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Summary

Christmas Coloring Book is a kids coloring app with Christmas drawings (tree, snowman, reindeer, penguin, gifts and more). The app works 100% offline and locally on your device: it never uploads drawings, chosen colors, or any information to external servers.

The app does not display ads and does not use Google AdMob or any other advertising network.

2. Information Processed

The app collects no personal data and requires no registration, login, or user account. Everything is processed on the device itself:

  • The drawings you choose and colors applied, processed locally for display and saving;
  • Drawings saved to the device photo gallery, only when you choose to save or share.

No drawing, image, manifest text, or report leaves your device.

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, contacts, photos, health data, location, or browsing history. We do not send crash logs, stack traces, performance metrics, or diagnostic telemetry.

3. How Information Is Used

As no personal data is collected, there is no use of information for marketing, profiling, or sharing with third parties.

4. Advertising

The app contains no ads and uses no advertising SDK. No advertising identifier (Advertising ID) is collected by the app.

5. Permissions

The app requests only:

  • Storage — to save your drawings to the device gallery (only when you choose to save).

6. Data Security

Because everything is processed locally, no data travels over the network. We do not store data on external servers.

7. Retention and Deletion

We maintain no user database or remote telemetry servers. Saved drawings remain only on your device and can be deleted by you at any time through the device gallery.

8. Children

This app is directed to children and we do not knowingly collect any data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Updates will be published on this page.
