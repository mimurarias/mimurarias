Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

🔒 Key Privacy Principles

At Mimurarias, user privacy is a core architectural foundation:

  • No accounts, logins, or signups: We do not require registration, passwords, full names, emails, or phone numbers to use our mobile apps;
  • On-device processing: Our technical utilities (e.g., APK AppSec, network ping tools) process data 100% on your device hardware without transmitting files or code to cloud servers;
  • No covert telemetry: We do not run background usage tracking or behavioral profiling trackers;
  • Transparent advertising: Free ad-supported apps utilize Google AdMob. Paid or ad-free versions contain zero advertising SDKs.

1. Overview and Scope

This unified Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, software tools, utilities, and games published under the Mimurarias brand (collectively, the "Apps"), distributed via the Google Play Store or authorized digital distribution channels.

This document explains clearly how data is handled across our Apps in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law No. 13,709/2018), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation 2016/679), and Google Play Developer Policies.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Mimurarias enforces a strict data minimization policy. Our servers never collect, store, or sell:

  • Your full name, national ID numbers, or date of birth;
  • Your email address or telephone number (unless you actively and voluntarily contact our support team);
  • Your phone contacts list or call logs;
  • Photos, photo galleries, personal videos, local documents, or voice recordings;
  • Precise geographic location data (real-time GPS coordinates);
  • Sensitive personal data, such as biometric identifiers, medical information, religious beliefs, or political opinions;
  • Account credentials, passwords, or web browsing history;
  • Crash logs, stack traces, or continuous device usage telemetry on our servers.

3. Local and Autonomous On-Device Operation

The core design of Mimurarias Apps ensures that computing tasks happen strictly within your local device hardware:

  • Security Audits (e.g., APK AppSec): The app statically inspects selected APK files or installed packages by reading binary manifests and compiled DEX bytecode in transient memory. No APK bytecode, secrets, or inspection reports are transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers;
  • Network Diagnostics (e.g., Network Ping Test): Latency tests and socket connections are performed directly from your device to the target host of your choice, without routing through or mirroring to Mimurarias servers;
  • Hardware Utilities (e.g., SOS Strobe Flashlight): The flashlight directly controls the hardware LED via the standard Android CameraManager API, without initializing camera video preview or capturing media;
  • Casual Games (e.g., Tic-Tac-Toe, arcade games): Game logic, high scores, and settings reside in the local private app sandbox on your device.

4. Third-Party Advertising Networks (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and offer tools free of charge, certain free versions of our Apps display banner and interstitial ads powered by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited).

4.1. Data Processed by the Google AdMob SDK

When using a free ad-supported app, the Google AdMob SDK may automatically process certain technical device information strictly to deliver, measure, and protect against fraudulent ad impressions, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID): Used by Google for ad measurement, frequency capping, and ad personalization (when consented to by the user);
  • IP Address: Used by Google for approximate geographic location (city or country level) and click fraud prevention;
  • Technical Device & Network Info: Device model, operating system version, carrier, and creative display performance metrics.

4.2. Google Privacy Policies and Terms

All data processing conducted by Google is governed directly by Google's privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4.3. Paid and Ad-Free Versions

In paid versions or apps configured without advertisements, the Google AdMob SDK is completely excluded or disabled, and no ad network requests are made.

5. Android Permissions and Purpose

Our Apps request only permissions strictly required to deliver the features you choose to use. Examples of requested permissions include:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to perform user-initiated network diagnostic tests or to receive ad creatives via Google AdMob;
  • FLASHLIGHT / CAMERA: Exclusively used to toggle the hardware flash LED in flashlight utilities, without capturing or transmitting imagery;
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Requested exclusively in specialized cybersecurity and audit tools (such as APK AppSec) to allow you to select an installed package on your own device for local static security auditing;
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Requested on modern Android versions only when you trigger a long-running foreground task (such as continuous ping monitoring with a status notification);
  • AD_ID / Google Ad Services Permissions: Required by Google Play policies for apps utilizing advertising SDKs on Android 13+.

Permissions are never leveraged to covertly monitor the user outside the app's explicit operational context.

6. Data Security and Storage

Mimurarias does not operate centralized databases containing browsing histories, logs, or user identities. Generated local data is safeguarded by Android's application sandbox security model.

When external network communication occurs (e.g., Google AdMob ad delivery requests or global web game leaderboards), all data in transit is encrypted using modern HTTPS/TLS protocols.

7. Data Retention and How to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID

Because we do not store identifiable personal data on central servers, there are no cumbersome account deletion procedures required:

  • Deleting Local Data: Uninstalling the app from your device or clearing app data via Android Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear data immediately purges all local settings and preferences;
  • Resetting or Deleting your Advertising ID: On your Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time by going to Settings > Google > All services > Ads (or Privacy > Ads) and selecting "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID". You may also opt out of personalized ads.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K / Google Play Families)

General Mimurarias Apps are intended for a broad audience and are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If any Mimurarias app is enrolled in the Google Play Families program, strict standards apply: no personalized behavioral advertising is served, and all integrated SDKs comply strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and European GDPR-K.

9. User Rights (LGPD, GDPR, and Global Regulations)

Under the LGPD, GDPR, and comparable data protection frameworks worldwide, users retain the right to:

  • Confirm the existence of data processing;
  • Access clear information regarding any processed information;
  • Request correction, anonymization, blocking, or deletion of excessive data;
  • Revoke previously granted permissions and consents at any time;
  • Receive transparent disclosure regarding third-party service providers (such as Google AdMob).

Since Mimurarias does not maintain user accounts or personal profiles on remote servers, your control over device permissions and advertising identifiers is instantly exercisable directly through your Android device settings.
