Privacy Policy - Port Scanner: Network Tools

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Versão em português

1. Summary

Port Scanner probes the TCP ports of a host you type yourself (hostname, IPv4 address or domain) and shows which ones answer. It requires no sign-up, no login and no personal data from you.

To probe the ports, the app opens direct connections from your device to the target you typed. There is no developer server in between: connections leave your phone and go to the chosen host. Nothing is stored by the developer: there is no account, no user database and no scan history.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, described below. The one-time "remove ads" purchase (inside the app) turns all ads off.

2. Data the app handles

The app does not collect personal data for the developer. What exists is the traffic needed to probe the ports and to show ads:

  • Port scan — the app opens TCP connections to the ports of the host you typed. As with any network connection, the destination sees the originating public IP address, the date and the time. The developer neither receives nor records those connections.
  • Public IP lookup — to show "Public IP" in the network panel, the app queries an IP service (ipv4.icanhazip.com, api.ipify.org or checkip.amazonaws.com). The answer is shown on screen and is not stored.
  • Device network data — local IP, gateway and DNS servers are read from the system and stay in the app's memory only.
  • Advertising ID — used only by the Google AdMob SDK to serve and measure ads in the free version.
  • Ad interaction data — ad display, personalisation and measurement, also by AdMob.
  • In-app purchase — processed entirely by Google Play. The app stores a local flag on your device only, so it knows it must stop showing ads.

We do not collect name, email, phone, address, contacts, photos, files, health information, precise location or browsing history.

We do not send crash logs, stack traces, performance data or diagnostic telemetry. If the app crashes, nothing is reported anywhere.

3. How the information is used

The information is used exclusively to:

  • Probe the ports of the chosen target and show the result on screen
  • Show, measure and limit ads in the free version
  • Recognise the purchase that removes ads

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an app-open ad when you return to the app and an interstitial when a scan finishes. The Google SDK may collect the device advertising ID and ad interaction data, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policy.

Buying the ad removal stops ads and the AdMob SDK is no longer initialised.

5. Permissions

The app requests these permissions:

  • INTERNET — connect to the target ports, look up the public IP and load ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — read network information (local IP, gateway, DNS)
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — process the purchase that removes ads
  • AD_ID and Google Ad Services permissions — obtain the advertising ID, required by AdMob

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, SMS, precise location or phone access.

6. Data security

Public IP lookups and ads use HTTPS. Port probes are direct TCP connections to the target you chose. No credentials are stored on the device beyond the local purchase flag, and we keep no servers that receive information about who uses the app.

7. Responsible use

The app is a network diagnostic tool. Scan only hosts you own or are allowed to test. Scanning third-party networks without permission may violate their terms of use and applicable law. The developer has no access to what you scan or to the results.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep no database of user data. Scan history is not saved: closing the app clears the results on screen.

Access logs stay with the parties involved (the host you scanned, IP lookup services, Google AdMob, Google Play) under their own policies. You can reset the Advertising ID or limit tracking in your device settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the local purchase flag (the purchase itself stays in your Google Play account and can be restored by reinstalling the app).

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The app itself does not use cookies. The Google AdMob SDK may use device identifiers for advertising and measurement, as described in the Google policy linked in section 4.

10. Children

This app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not allow personalised ads to children under 13.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected on this page and in the app update.
