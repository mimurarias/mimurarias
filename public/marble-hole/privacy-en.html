Privacy Policy - Marble Hole

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Summary

Marble Hole is a board game (Boca de Gude) for one or two players. The app does not require registration, login, or any personal data from you.

2. Information Collected

The app does not collect personal data directly. The following information is processed by third parties:

  • Advertising ID - display and measurement of ads (free version)
  • Ad interaction data - display and measurement of ads (free version)

Game progress (wins, game mode, difficulty, chosen marble colors) is stored only locally on the device via SharedPreferences. Nothing is stored on external servers.

3. Use of Information

Information is used exclusively for:

  • Displaying and measuring ads (free version)
  • Persisting game preferences locally

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

The app does not contain any monitoring, analytics, or crash reporting SDK. There is no telemetry from us: nothing about your device, how you play, or match results is sent to any of our servers or any third party on our behalf. The only data leaving the device is described above, managed by Google AdMob.

4. Advertising

The free version displays Google AdMob ads in banner, app open, and interstitial (at match end) formats. The Google SDK may collect the device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, per the Google Privacy Policy and Google Ads Policy. Ads may be personalized based on your device and in-app activity.

The paid version (Marble Hole Pro) contains no ads and no AdMob SDK.

4.1. In-App Purchase (Remove Ads)

The free version offers a non-consumable purchase ("remove_ads") via Google Play Billing to permanently remove ads. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play Billing; the app only receives purchase confirmation and sets the "ads removed" flag in local preferences. We do not store credit card data, billing address, or any financial data.

4.2. Error and Crash Diagnostics

This app does not integrate any performance monitoring SDK. There is no APM agent, error tracking, or analytics inside the app: nothing about your device or how you use the app is transmitted by us.

When installed via Google Play, Google Play itself may collect error and crash reports (Android vitals) on the device so the developer can view aggregated crash statistics. This collection is done by Google Play independently of the app and does not involve any data you entered in the app. Retention rules and ways to limit this are controlled by Google per the Google Privacy Policy.

If you prefer not to send these diagnostic reports, you can disable the option in Google Play settings or device system settings.

5. Permissions

The app requests only three permissions:

  • INTERNET - required to load ads (free version) and process in-app purchases
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - required to check connectivity before loading ads
  • VIBRATE - required for haptic feedback during gameplay

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other personal data.

6. Data Security

Ad data and Advertising ID travel via Google AdMob SDK over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not store credentials or personal data on our servers, and we have no server of our own: the app is self-contained. The "remove_ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing with PCI-DSS security.

7. Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database with user data, and we have no server that stores anything about you. Ad data is retained by respective advertisers and the Google AdMob network per their policies. Android vitals crash reports are retained by Google per section 4.2. You can reset your Advertising ID, limit ad tracking on your device, or delete ad data in your device settings. To erase local game progress, simply clear the app data in Android settings.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies or store its own identifiers in device preferences. The Google AdMob SDK may use device identifiers for advertising and analytics purposes per Google's policy linked in section 4.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not allow personalized ads to users under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via app update or this page.
