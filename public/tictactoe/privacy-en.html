Privacy Policy - Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Summary

Tic Tac Toe is a tic-tac-toe game. The free version displays ads through Google AdMob. The paid version contains no ads and no AdMob SDK. The game works offline; the free version only needs internet to load ads.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, files, health information, precise location, or browsing history. None of this information is sent to any third party.

2. Information Collection and Use

The app does not collect personal data directly and does not run a server. The information below is processed by Google through the AdMob SDK:

  • Advertising ID — ad display and measurement
  • Ad interaction data — ad display, personalization, and measurement
  • Approximate location — derived from the IP address by AdMob, for ad targeting

The app does not send crash logs, stack traces, performance data, or diagnostic telemetry. If the game crashes, that is not reported anywhere.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, files, health information, precise location, or browsing history.

3. Use of Information

Information is used exclusively to display, measure, and limit ads.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Advertising

The free version displays ads from Google AdMob, in banner format on the main screen and as app-open ads when you return to the app. The Google SDK may collect your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data, per the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policy. Ads may be personalized based on your device and activity in the app.

The paid version of the app contains no ads and no AdMob SDK.

5. Permissions

The app requests these permissions:

  • INTERNET — to load ads
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check connectivity before requesting an ad

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other personal data.

6. Data Security

Ad traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not store credentials or personal data on servers of our own.

7. Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a database of user data or game history. Ad data is retained by Google AdMob and the respective advertisers under their own policies.

You can reset your Advertising ID or limit tracking in your device settings.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies. The Google AdMob SDK may use device identifiers for advertising and measurement purposes, as described in the Google policy linked in section 4.

9. Children

This app is not directed to children or teenagers under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. We do not allow personalized ads for children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through an app update or on this page.
