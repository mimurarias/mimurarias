Privacy Policy - Geo Tools

Last update: September 26, 2026

1. Overview

Geo Tools is a utility suite for cartographic and surveying engineers (Precision Leveling NBR 13133, Coordinate Converter, Digital Bubble Level, and GPS Quick-Point). It operates on an offline-first architecture and does not require registration, login, or any personal data input.

The free version of the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (banner and app-open ads), which may use the device's advertising identifier. All data generated by the tools (calculations, coordinate lists, bubble logbooks, and GPS collections) is stored exclusively in your device's local database (SQLite) and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Information Collected

The app does not collect personal data directly. The only information processed by third parties (Google AdMob) relates to:

  • Advertising ID - for displaying and measuring ads in the free version.
  • Ad interaction data - ad delivery, personalization, and measurement.

For the GPS Quick-Point tool, the app requests fine location (GPS) permission strictly to obtain current device coordinates in real-time, without uploading location history to external servers (all storage is strictly local).

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, contacts, photos, files, or health information. We do not transmit telemetry or stack traces to external servers.

3. Use of Information

Advertising information is used exclusively by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads in the free version. Survey and coordinate data entered by the user remain strictly on the device.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via support email.
